ABOUT RECALL

A smarter way to stay on top of your academics.

Recall is a student-focused academic productivity platform designed to make learning more organized, manageable, and effective.

Make room for what matters: learning.

From tracking courses and organizing lecture notes to creating flashcards, managing assignments, and planning revision sessions, Recall brings your academic activities together in one place.

Our goal is simple: help students spend less time trying to organize their studies and more time actually learning.
